Introduction about Panda Fries

Panda Fries is a food company in Lahore, Pakistan, that offers fries in a variety of flavors. Panda Fries serves tons of fries to fries lovers.

Panda Fries has the following products.

Panda Fries Straight Cuts
Panda Fries 7mm Cuts
Panda Fries Crinkle Cuts
King Fries Straight Cuts
Patatas Fries Straight Cuts
Golden Cuts Fries Defrost

We make fries for fries lovers. Our first priority is our customers' satisfaction. We have done everything to make Panda Fries the best. If we get any complaint regarding our products, we take immediate action and make sure the mistake never repeats. We don't claim to be perfect, but we improve every day.

Types of Potatoes

There are many types of potatoes, but the two most commonly used in Panda Fries are:

  • AVR
  • LR
We use two types of potatoes: AVR and LR. All our potatoes are produced in Pakistan.
Potatoes are bought from different areas and cities.
AVR is the most commonly used in Panda Fries. AVR has the best taste, color, and size, which makes the fries top quality.
We ensure proper hygiene from start to end.
In a few years, Panda Fries will become an international brand.
Panda Fries is now leading the fries industry all over Pakistan.

Marketing

There are various strategies for marketing, but Panda Fries mostly follows these two:

  • Direct Marketing
  • Indirect Marketing

Direct Marketing

Panda Fries has a direct marketing deal with PTV. It is also on social media with official accounts and a website. Panda Fries has deals with multiple shopping 🛒 malls, which helps increase its market.

Indirect Marketing

Panda Fries also does indirect marketing with salesmen going shop to shop and giving free tasting. This kind of strategy builds a strong connection between customers and the company.

Annual Function

Panda Fries holds an annual function every year. Annual function day is a holiday in the company. The function is organized by the owner.
Every employee is invited by the company. Everyone loves to participate because they get a chance to interact with senior management and the owner in a good environment.
At the annual function, the company gives rewards for the best performance of the year. At the end, everyone enjoys delicious dishes.
